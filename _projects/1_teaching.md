A college algebra course with emphasis on functions. Topics include: properties of the real number system, equations and inequalities, lines, graphs, algebra of functions, modeling, exponents, logarithms, systems of equations and conic sections, and introduction to trigonometry.

Taught in:

- Fall 2022 at Wayne State University
- Fall 2023 at Wayne State University
